## Style Prompt

AgriFarm's short video should feel like a warm civic product reveal: premium, minimal, organic, and credible enough for a thesis panel and Pasig stakeholders. The frame should feel like a modern launch film for a local food system, not a school report. Use spacious layouts, large confident typography, soft botanical texture, and product screenshots or illustrations as hero objects.

## Colors

- Background: `#FFF7E8` warm cream for human, food, and civic warmth.
- Deep foreground: `#14260E` leaf-black for premium contrast.
- Primary green: `#145C2A` for AgriFarm identity and calls to action.
- Harvest gold: `#D99A3A` for emphasis, forecast highlights, and light leaks.
- Clay brown: `#6B3F22` for soil, trust, and civic grounding.

## Typography

- Display voice: a high-contrast serif stack, 800-900 weight for launch headlines and emotional lines.
- Interface/data voice: `IBM Plex Mono` or a monospace fallback, 500-700 weight for labels, sample-data notes, and forecast readouts.
- Body voice: system sans, 300-700 weight for supporting copy.

## Motion

- Calm premium energy with focus-pull, warm light leak, and soft push transitions.
- Entrance animations should vary: type rises, image panels scale and pan, data bars draw, map pins pulse.
- Avoid PowerPoint-style slide-ins; transitions should feel like camera and editorial movement.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use cold neon tech colors, purple gradients, or generic SaaS blue.
- Do not show dense paragraphs or academic report slides.
- Do not imply the prototype contains real production data.
- Do not center every scene with identical card grids.
